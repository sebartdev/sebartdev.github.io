PackPal

A packing list for every trip. Pick your activities, tick things off, and leave home without forgetting anything.

Coming soon to Google Play

What it does

  • ActivitiesBeach, pool, abroad, mountains and more add their items to one list, sorted by where they go in your luggage.
  • Trip lengthBasics for a day, a short or a long trip; quantities can follow the number of days.
  • Quick tickingTap to pack, set quantities with a stepper, see progress for every category.
  • Before you leave homeWindows, lights, water, gas – a separate checklist for the moment you lock the door.
  • SuppliesKeep track of what you have at home or at your second place.
  • Light and darkEnglish and Polish, light and dark theme.

Your data stays yours

No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. PackPal does not even have permission to use the internet – everything you enter stays on your phone. Privacy policy

Co potrafi

  • AktywnościPlaża, basen, zagranica, góry i inne dokładają rzeczy do jednej listy, ułożonej według miejsca w bagażu.

  • Przed wyjściem z domuOkna, światła, woda, gaz – osobna lista na moment zamykania drzwi.
  • ZapasyWiesz, ile czego masz w domu albo na stancji.
